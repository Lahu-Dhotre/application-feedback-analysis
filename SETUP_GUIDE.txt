================================================================================
CUSTOMIZATION OPTIONS
================================================================================

To customize the analysis:

1. Dataset Size: Modify total_records in Cell 2
   Current: 570 records
   Change: total_records = 1000

2. Rating Distribution: Adjust rating_distribution in Cell 2
   Current: {5: 0.30, 4: 0.35, 3: 0.20, 2: 0.10, 1: 0.05}

3. Feedback Content: Add custom feedback templates in Cell 2
   templates are organized by rating and application

4. Sentiment Threshold: Adjust categorization in Cell 5
   Current: Positive ≥ 0.3, Negative ≤ -0.3
